B · Matching: role card

What changes, in short

  • The purple AI bar moves from the left side to the right column.
  • New Overall match under Skill Match: Skill Match × (100% − AI touch) + AI know-how × AI touch.
  • AI touch comes from data: Moderate = share of the job's tasks seen in real AI use (Anthropic Economic Index); Rapid = ILO exposure score.
  • Skill Match never changes. Overall can change when you switch Moderate ↔ Rapid.
  • No AI data → Overall = Skill Match. AI questions not answered → no Overall, a button to answer them.
  • Roles are sorted by Overall match when it exists.
