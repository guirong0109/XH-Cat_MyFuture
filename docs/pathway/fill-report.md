## Fields of study

How every field below was searched on 2026-10-31:

- **MQR** (Malaysian Qualifications Register, www2.mqa.gov.my/mqr): the search form posted by script with every field (programme code, name and institution left empty, NEC field set, all modes, all levels, all states). Accredited certificates, diplomas, bachelor's, master's and doctoral programmes all count. The NEC field is the one whose name matches the ISCED field: NEC and ISCED-F names were compared for all 51 fields searched; they match for 49, and for ISCED 0611 (Computer use) and 0712 (Environmental protection technology) the matching NEC fields 0614 and 0717 were used. NEC codes read like ISCED-F codes but are not the same everywhere: MQR numbers 0611–0614, 0712–0717 and 0731–0734 differently (e.g. NEC 0712 is Electricity and energy, NEC 0716 Civil engineering, NEC 0732 Urban and Regional Planning).
- **MOHE e-Panduan UPU** (online.mohe.gov.my/epanduan): reachable, but its programme search is a script form by programme name; posted by script it returned no result list, so it could not be used.
- **University programme lists** scanned for the field's words: UTM admission programme list (admission.utm.my/new2024/programmes, 14 pages), UPM undergraduate list (upm.edu.my/admission/programmes/undergraduate-75800), UKM Find Your Programme (ukm.my/studyukm/find-your-programme). The UMS bachelor list (ums.edu.my/v5/en/bachelor-programme) returned 404.
- For each MQR programme that could fit, the institution's own site was searched for a programme page whose heading names the programme. A field stays here when MQR has no programme for it, or when no institution page with such a heading could be opened.
